COMBAT MANUAL

1. The Basics

Use [UP / DOWN ARROWS] to slip between the 3 lanes.
[LEFT ARROW] = Ghost Step (Emergency evade, resets Combo)
[A] [A] [A] = Jab Chain (Builds Combo, safe 3rd hit shove)
[S] = Cross (Heavy hitstop, Stuns, Armor Break, Cashes out Combo)
[D] = Hook (Space-maker, Cashes out Combo)
[SHIFT] = Guard (Reduces damage by 75%)

2. Reading The Lanes (Perfect Slips)

When an enemy is preparing to attack, their lane will flash RED. Slipping now grants a Good Slip.
When the lane flashes BRIGHT WHITE, you are in the Perfect Slip Window.
A Perfect Slip completely evades the attack, grants a massive Instinct Meter boost, and CHARGES a devastating Counter Hit for your next strike!

3. Guard Offense & Breaking Shields

Enemies with GOLD ARMOR will block Jabs. You MUST use a CROSS [S] to shatter their shield.
While holding GUARD [SHIFT], you can press [A] for a fast Guard Jab, or [D] for a space-making Check Hook. These are safe, but won't build Combo.

4. Combo Strings & Branching

Landing consecutive Jabs [A] builds your Combo.
Branching into a CROSS [S] or HOOK [D] mid-string will CASH OUT your Combo, converting it into massive damage, stun, and launch power!
Leaving the lane, guarding, ghost stepping, or missing an attack will reset the combo back to zero. Target switching weakens your cashouts.

5. Instinct Mode

When the top meter is full, press [SPACE] to unleash Instinct Mode.
You gain Hyper Armor, take 50% less damage, and your raw attacks gain full knockback. Control the crowd!
