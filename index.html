COMBAT MANUAL

1. The Basics

[UP / DOWN] slip between the 3 lanes. [L-SHIFT] Ghost Step (a short invincible dash — its own button). [LEFT / RIGHT] held = footwork: give ground / press forward.
[A] [A] [A] Jab chain  ·  [S] Cross (armor break)  ·  [D] Hook (space-maker)  ·  [W] hold to Guard  ·  [SPACE] Instinct.
Every key can be remapped in Pause → Settings → Controls. Pad: X Jab · Y Cross · B Hook · LB Ghost Step · RB Guard · A Instinct.

2. Footwork & Spacing

Hold [RIGHT] to press forward and meet them early; hold [LEFT] to give ground and buy a beat. You hold your position while you're fighting — with nobody in reach you drift back to neutral on your own.
Some blows can't be slipped: a Bruiser's SWEEP covers its lane and both neighbours. Ghost Step [L-SHIFT] straight through it, or Guard [W] and eat a fraction of it.

3. Reading The Lanes (Perfect Slips)

When an enemy is preparing to attack, their lane will flash RED. Slipping now grants a Good Slip.
When the lane flashes BRIGHT WHITE, you are in the Perfect Slip Window.
A Perfect Slip completely evades the attack, grants a massive Instinct Meter boost, and CHARGES a devastating Counter Hit for your next strike!

4. Guard Offense & Breaking Shields

Enemies with GOLD ARMOR will block Jabs. You MUST use a CROSS [S] to shatter their defense.
While holding GUARD [W], you can press [A] for a fast Guard Jab, or [D] for a space-making Check Hook. These are safe, but won't build Combo.

5. Know Your Opponent

Bruisers shrug off Jabs entirely — only a Cross (or a Counter Hit) staggers them, though Jabs still chip their health. Assassins bite through Guard almost completely — turtling barely works on them, they have to be read and slipped. Zoners fire from range on a visible countdown; slip out of their lane before it fires.

6. Pressure & Cash-Out

Landing any punch pins Pressure onto that target — shown as small pink pips above its health bar, up to 3.
Follow up with a CROSS [S] before it decays and the built-up pressure converts straight into bonus damage: a real payoff for a Jab string, not just chip damage.
Pressure fades on its own if you don't cash it out in time — bank it now, or keep building and risk losing it all.

7. Instinct & The Zone

When the Instinct meter is full, press [SPACE] for Instinct Mode: half damage taken, double knockback. Or keep it banked and land a Perfect Slip on a full meter: you drop into THE ZONE — the screen inverts and the world slows to a crawl while you move at full speed.

8. Evolutions

Fill the pink EXP bar and you evolve: choose one of three. Earn several at once and you pick them back to back, then see them all together.
Each tree — SPEED, POWER, TECHNIQUE — climbs five ranks as the run goes deeper. Rank 3 is the tree's Signature Move, rank 5 its Apex:
Pivot Slip (Speed): slips carry you forward and your next punch is instant. Loaded Cross (Power): hold Cross to load a guard-breaking blow, release to throw. Afterimage Slip (Technique): a Perfect Slip leaves an afterimage that throws an echo punch.
Two trees at rank 3 unlock their Fusion; both at rank 5 perfect it. Pause → Loadout explains every evolution.

Defeating enemies and landing Perfect Slips charges your Evolution meter (the pink bar, top right).
When it fills, the next lull in combat opens a draft of three upgrades — pick with [1] [2] [3] or [X] [Y] [B].
Max a tree to unlock Masteries; combine two maxed trees to unlock run-defining Fusions.

9. Adversaries

Every archetype asks one distinct question — learn the question, not the enemy.
Grunt — the metronome. Reads clean, dies clean. Sets your rhythm.
Shield — the wall. Jabs bounce; only a Cross [S] opens it.
Bruiser — the freight. Shrugs off jabs entirely (they still chip), keeps swinging through your pressure. Cross, Counter, or True Read to stagger. Leave it alive and it HARDENS — faster, meaner — so deal with it early.
Assassin — the needle. Bites through Guard (~60% still lands). Turtling fails; you must read and slip.
Zoner — the rail. Fires down a lane on a visible countdown. Leave the lane before it commits — and the longer it lives, the faster it charges.

The Bosses escalate their read as you climb the Arcs:
Neon Enforcer — armored pressure. Punishes jab-spam into its guard, harder each Arc.
Phantom Boxer — the feint. Shifts lanes, and in later Arcs throws decoy tells and afterimages that cost you only if you take the bait.
Static Monk — the zoner king. Chains longer laser volleys and, deep in, calls support while it recharges.
Live Wire — the pressure fighter. Punch strings, walks you back, and every string leaves the lane he worked LIVE for a beat — change lanes or fry.
Negative — your inverted mirror, and the last fight. Punch it when it isn't OPEN and it slips you with an afterimage, or reads your Counter and answers it. Bait, slip, then punish.

10. Boss Duels: Open & Finishers

Every boss attack is announced: a sound, a windup ring filling over its head, and its lane turning red. Slip or guard it — then it slumps OPEN (cyan brackets). Hits on an OPEN boss land harder, and its armor and Phantom Shift can't answer them. Hitting it when it isn't open is what gets punished.
Knock a boss past 66% and 33% health (the white notches on its bar) and it STAGGERS into a Finisher: hit each prompt on the beat as the ring closes. Mashing early or pressing the wrong move ends the stagger — nothing worse. The killing blow opens a final KO Finisher.

11. Score & Wagers

Score is earned live (top of screen) and multiplied by your Combo (+0.25× every 5). Perfect Slips, perfect Ghost Steps, punishes and Finishers pay the most. A perfect Ghost Step — dashing through a live attack — adds +1 Combo.
Before most stages the arena offers a Stage Wager: accept a modifier for a score multiplier on that stage, or decline and play it clean.

12. Punch Strings & The Ten-Count

From Arc 2, some enemies throw 2-3 punch strings (pips over their head count them). Every punch follows you into your new lane with its own red → white tell — slip each one.
At 0 HP you go DOWN, not out — once per arc. The ref counts to ten: hit the lane prompts before ten to get up — the cleaner your timing, the more health you get up with. Go down twice in one arc and the fight is over.
